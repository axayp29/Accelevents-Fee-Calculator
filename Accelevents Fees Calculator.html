How each ticket is calculated

  1. Flat fee per ticket = flat ÷ paid ticket types ÷ quantity (SEPA €0.35; ACH has no flat fee).
  2. Fee on buyer: amount grossed up as amount ÷ (1 − fee %). If that fee is more than the cap still left, the buyer pays amount + cap left instead.
  3. Absorb fee: the buyer pays only the price; the organizer's fee is fee % × price.
  4. Sales tax (on top) is worked out on the price plus the capped fee, so tax is never charged on fee the buyer doesn't pay.
  5. VAT is set per ticket type: that ticket's VAT % × ticket price (never on the fee), added on top. When the fee is passed to the buyer, the processing fee also covers the VAT amount.
  6. The ticket's fee is limited to the cap left, then subtracted from it. The next ticket sees what remains; once the $5 is used, later tickets pay $0.
  7. Credit card is 2.9% + $0.30 flat per order by default (both editable), with the same gross-up and no cap. Card, SEPA and free tickets never touch the cap.
